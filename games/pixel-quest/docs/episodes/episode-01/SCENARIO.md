# Episode 01 — Первый сбой

## Purpose

Episode 01 is the first playable vertical slice of:

**PIXEL QUEST: НЕ ИГРА**

The episode introduces the unknown digital character without explaining its origin.

---

# Player Objective

Investigate an unexpected system signal and determine what caused it.

---

# Narrative Result

By the end of the episode the player should understand:

* an unknown character exists;
* the character is not registered normally;
* somebody attempted to delete it;
* deletion failed;
* the character became active again;
* it obtained administrator access;
* it can communicate directly with players;
* it can interfere with the Pixel Quest system.

---

# Questions That Must Remain Open

The player must NOT yet know:

* who created the character;
* why it exists;
* where its admin access came from;
* what happened to the developers;
* where the developers are;
* what Back Logs are;
* what Protocol 17 really is;
* whether the character is dangerous;
* what it ultimately wants.

---

# Target Duration

Production target:

**5–8 minutes**

Technical prototype:

**3–5 minutes**

---

# Gameplay Objects

Episode contains:

* 3 AR markers;
* 1 unknown character;
* 1 system terminal;
* 1 corrupted character file;
* system UI;
* dialogue UI.
