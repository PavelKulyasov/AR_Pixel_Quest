# Protocol 17

## Internal Canon

Protocol 17 is NOT the ID of the Anomaly.

Protocol 17 is a special emergency mechanism associated with deleting or neutralizing an autonomous digital object that has obtained developer-level permissions.

It existed as a failsafe.

At some point an attempt was made to use it.

The attempt did not produce the expected result.

---

# Player Knowledge

During Episode 01 players must NOT receive a complete explanation of Protocol 17.

At most, indirect references may eventually appear.

The intended future reveal is based on the distinction:

```text
OBJECT ID: UNKNOWN

PROTOCOL: 17
```

Players may initially incorrectly assume that "17" identifies the character.

Later they should discover that Protocol 17 refers to the mechanism intended to remove it.

---

# Important Mystery

Do not fully explain:

* who created Protocol 17;
* when it was created;
* why it was numbered 17;
* exactly what happened during activation;
* whether the protocol failed or was deliberately blocked.

These questions can support later episodes and sequels.
